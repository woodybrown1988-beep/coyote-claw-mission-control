Recommended data architecture

Mission Control combines timestamps and exceptions; each source remains authoritative for its own events

Lightspeed K POS

Orders, checks, covers, order profiles, users, voids, refunds, corrections, open orders and production-centre routing.

ORDER & TRANSACTION SOURCE

Lightspeed KDS

Products prepared, fastest/slowest/average preparation time and ticket completion events by production centre.

KITCHEN TIMING SOURCE

OpenTable

Arrival, seating, status, table assignment, party size, configured/actual turn times, waitlist and occupancy.

FOH FLOW SOURCE

LivePepper / StoreKit

Order received, promise time, accepted, ready, collection, customer identity and channel-specific failures.

DIGITAL ORDER SOURCE

Labour module

Kitchen and FOH scheduled/actual hours and role deployment for throughput and shift-score context.

STAFFING CONTEXT

Inventory module

Product availability, stockouts, remakes, recorded waste and ingredient constraints.

AVAILABILITY CONTEXT

Reservations & Reviews

Guest visits, reviews, service-recovery follow-up and whether affected guests return.

GUEST OUTCOME SOURCE

Safety app

Critical food-safety, allergen and equipment exceptions used as hard shift-score overrides.
