Recommended data architecture

One cost ledger, multiple operational sources, no duplicate truth

Lightspeed K-Series

Sales mix, item quantities, recipe depletion, stock levels, stock movements, purchase orders, suppliers, purchased quantities and cost prices.

THEORETICAL & INVENTORY SOURCE

Supplier invoices / statements

Line-level pack size, usable-unit price, tax, delivery charges, credits, substitutions, invoice dates and payment terms.

PURCHASE PRICE SOURCE

QuickBooks

Chart of accounts, bills, expense accruals, fixed costs, accounts payable, cash payment timing, budget and financial reconciliation.

Revenue & Labour modules

Forecast/actual net revenue and fully loaded labour budgets/costs, enabling prime cost, break-even and site contribution.
