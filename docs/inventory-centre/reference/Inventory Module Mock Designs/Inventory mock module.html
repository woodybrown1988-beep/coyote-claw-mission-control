Oversight rules

What management should review and when.

Daily: high-risk flash counts, stockout risk, unposted waste and batches.\n\nWeekly: full count, actual vs theoretical, top variances, waste, below-par items, open POs and credits.\n\nMonthly: stock turns, dead stock, supplier price movement, recipe/yield audit and independent recount sample.\n\nQuarterly: supplier tendering, par redesign, menu complexity and stock-location review.
A weekly count is the minimum for reliable weekly COGS. Daily full counts are unnecessary; daily flash counts should focus on the few items carrying most value and variance risk.
