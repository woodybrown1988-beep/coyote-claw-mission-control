Permitted-contact logic

Campaign eligibility must be explicit and auditable
Eligible for channel = valid contact detail AND permitted lawful route for that channel AND no opt-out/objection AND campaign purpose matches permission AND frequency cap not exceeded AND no complaint/safeguarding exclusion\n\nSoft opt-in requires: details obtained directly during a sale/negotiation, marketing of similar products/services, clear opt-out at collection and an opt-out in every message.
Retain a suppression record rather than deleting every trace of an opt-out; otherwise a later import can accidentally re-add the person.
