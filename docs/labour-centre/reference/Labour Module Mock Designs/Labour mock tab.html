How the bands should be calibrated
Historical evidence should replace generic targets
For every historical week: 1. classify actual net revenue into a band 2. calculate fully loaded Kitchen and FOH labour % 3. exclude closures, launch weeks and corrupted clocking 4. compare labour, ticket times, reviews and guest waits 5. target the efficient frontier—not simply the lowest cost
A week is efficient only when labour is controlled without deterioration in service, food safety, quality or manager workload.

Labour accounting rules
Required for reliable comparisons
Labour cost includes: base wage/salary allocation + employer payroll on-costs + employer pension + paid leave / holiday provision + overtime premium + agency labour Productive hours exclude: paid leave, sickness, training and meetings unless the analysis explicitly concerns paid-hour utilisation
Tips or tronc should not be mixed into controllable employer labour cost unless the business funds them.
